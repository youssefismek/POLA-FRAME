How it works

Three steps, no learning curve.

  1. Add your photo

    Pick a photo from your phone or computer. JPG, PNG and WebP all work.

  2. Pick your look

    Choose a film filter and paper, then write a caption in handwriting-style fonts.

  3. Download in HD

    Save a high-resolution PNG, ready to post, share or print.

FAQ

Quick answers before you start.

Is POLA Frame free?

Yes. You can edit and download your prints for free, with no account.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. POLA Frame works in mobile browsers on iPhone and Android, with no app to install.

What can I use the prints for?

Instagram and Pinterest posts, scrapbooks, birthday and wedding cards, mood boards, or printing them at home.

Which photo formats can I use?

JPG, PNG and WebP images. Downloads are high-resolution PNG files.
